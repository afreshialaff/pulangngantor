# Storyboard Ramayana Part 2: Rama (awatara Wisnu sampai menjelang penobatan)

Kelas: fakta = sejarah/naskah; kisah = isi kitab (catatan kepercayaan); versi = tradisi lain.

## S01 · Cold open (kisah)
- VO: Dewa penjaga semesta bisa jadi apa saja. Tapi dalam kitab Ramayana, ia memilih lahir sebagai manusia. Bisa lapar, bisa lelah, bisa terluka. Kenapa?
- Visual: A majestic cosmic vision: the god Vishnu, serene, four-armed, deep blue skin, golden crown, reclining on the coiled many-headed celestial serpent on a shimmering ocean of stars, looking down toward a small glowing blue Earth where a tiny golden city twinkles
- Karakter: -
- Kamera: very slow push-in from the cosmic ocean toward Vishnu's calm face, then a gentle tilt down to the blue Earth

## S02 · Cold open (kisah)
- VO: Ingat Rahwana, raja raksasa di bagian pertama? Ia meminta kebal dari dewa dan raksasa. Manusia ia anggap terlalu lemah untuk disebut. Di situlah celahnya.
- Visual: Inside a dark volcanic mountain throne hall, the ten-headed demon king Rahwana, ten crowned heads, red and gold robes, laughs proudly beside a glowing divine boon flame; at the foot of the steps a tiny lone human archer sits in a shaft of golden light
- Karakter: -
- Kamera: slow dolly-in with a slight low angle on Rahwana

## S03 · Cold open (kisah)
- VO: Para dewa pun mengadu. Menurut Walmiki, penyair kitab ini, Dewa Wisnu bersedia turun sebagai manusia. Putra seorang raja. Namanya kelak Rama.
- Visual: Heavenly court above golden clouds: a group of worried gods with soft halos bow with folded hands before Vishnu, who raises one hand in gentle blessing while the great eagle Garuda spreads golden wings behind him
- Karakter: -
- Kamera: slow crane up through the clouds toward Vishnu

## S04 · Peta (fakta)
- VO: Kita akan jawab tiga hal. Bagaimana Rama lahir dan tumbuh. Apa yang menguji kekuatannya. Dan kenapa seluruh kota begitu percaya padanya.
- Visual: Triptych-like single composition: Rama standing centre on a hilltop at dawn, behind him in soft light the golden city of Ayodhya on the left, a dark mysterious forest in the middle distance, and a distant royal hall with a giant bow on the right
- Karakter: RAMA
- Kamera: slow arc of about fifteen degrees around Rama

## S05 · Catatan sumber (fakta)
- VO: Satu catatan jujur dulu. Banyak sarjana menilai kitab pertama Walmiki ditambahkan belakangan. Jadi masa kecil Rama ini kisah tradisi, bukan sejarah yang terbukti.
- Visual: A quiet ancient scriptorium at night: weathered palm-leaf manuscripts with abstract unreadable marks spread on a low wooden table, an old scholar's hands gently lifting one separate fresher leaf apart from the older stack, oil lamp glow, shelves of manuscript bundles
- Karakter: -
- Kamera: slow macro push-in toward the palm leaf

## S06 · Kelahiran (kisah)
- VO: Cerita dimulai di Ayodhya, ibu kota kerajaan Kosala. Rajanya, Dasarata, punya istana megah dan tiga permaisuri. Tapi ada satu yang kosong: ia belum punya putra.
- Visual: Dasarata stands alone on a palace balcony at golden hour overlooking the splendid city of Ayodhya with white towers, gardens and the Sarayu river; beside him an empty small golden cradle, melancholic expression
- Karakter: DASARATA
- Kamera: slow dolly from the empty cradle to Dasarata and the city

## S07 · Kelahiran (kisah)
- VO: Atas saran penasihatnya, sang raja mengundang Resyasringa, seorang pertapa muda. Ia memimpin upacara api yang besar, khusus untuk memohon lahirnya putra.
- Visual: A grand outdoor fire ritual courtyard: a young gentle sage with a small single horn on his forehead, simple white cloth, pours ghee into a large square sacrificial fire while Dasarata and three queens in colourful saris sit with folded hands, priests, marigold garlands
- Karakter: DASARATA
- Kamera: steady slow push-in toward the fire altar

## S08 · Kelahiran (kisah)
- VO: Dari kobaran api, muncul sosok bercahaya membawa bejana emas. Isinya bubur suci. Hadiah dari para dewa, katanya, untuk para permaisuri.
- Visual: From the roaring sacred fire rises a radiant tall celestial being made of warm golden light, offering a shining golden vessel of white sweet rice pudding with both hands to the astonished king Dasarata who kneels
- Karakter: DASARATA
- Kamera: slow tilt up following the rising being of light

## S09 · Kelahiran (kisah)
- VO: Separuh bubur untuk Kausalya. Sisanya dibagi untuk Sumitra dan Kaikeyi, dan Sumitra mendapat dua kali. Tak lama, ketiganya mengandung.
- Visual: In a candle-lit inner palace chamber Dasarata serves white pudding from the golden vessel into three silver bowls for three queens seated side by side: Kausalya in ivory, a second queen in emerald green, a third queen in sunset orange; the first bowl visibly the fullest
- Karakter: DASARATA, KAUSALYA
- Kamera: slow lateral slide from left to right across the three queens

## S10 · Kelahiran (kisah)
- VO: Di musim semi, Kausalya melahirkan Rama. Kaikeyi melahirkan Bharata. Sumitra melahirkan si kembar, Laksmana dan Satrugna. Satu istana, empat tangisan bayi.
- Visual: A joyful spring morning palace nursery: four newborn babies in four carved golden cradles arranged in a gentle arc, one baby with soft blue-tinged dusky skin in the front cradle, three queens smiling, flower petals falling through tall arches, blossoming trees outside
- Karakter: KAUSALYA
- Kamera: slow glide forward past the four cradles

## S11 · Masa kecil (kisah)
- VO: Rama dan Laksmana tak terpisahkan. Menurut Walmiki, Rama susah tidur tanpa adiknya. Makanan enak pun tak ia sentuh, kalau Laksmana belum ada.
- Visual: Night in a royal bedroom: two little princes with black hair sleep peacefully side by side on one large silk bed, little blue-tinged Rama in a yellow dhoti in front and little Lakshmana in a blue dhoti just behind him, a small candle and a plate of sweets on the side table, a full moon outside the arched balcony, rich curtains
- Karakter: -
- Kamera: very slow push-in toward the sleeping boys

## S12 · Kisah unik (versi)
- VO: Ada kisah unik dari Tulsidas, penyair India abad keenam belas. Seekor gagak, yang sebenarnya resi sakti, datang menonton Rama kecil bermain di halaman.
- Visual: Sunny marble palace courtyard with fountain and mango trees: little Rama sitting on the floor playing, a glossy black crow with intelligent golden shimmering eyes lands a few steps away watching him, Kausalya smiling from a doorway
- Karakter: RAMA_KECIL
- Kamera: gentle low dolly toward the crow and the child

## S13 · Kisah unik (versi)
- VO: Rama kecil mencoba menangkapnya. Gagak itu terbang jauh, sampai ke alam para dewa. Tapi tangan mungil Rama selalu tinggal sejengkal di belakangnya.
- Visual: High above clouds in a glowing celestial sky: the black crow flies swiftly with wings spread while a giant softly translucent small child's hand of little Rama reaches just a few finger widths behind it, golden heavenly palaces far below
- Karakter: RAMA_KECIL
- Kamera: smooth tracking shot following the crow through the clouds

## S14 · Kisah unik (versi)
- VO: Lalu sang gagak masuk ke mulut Rama. Di dalamnya ia melihat matahari, bulan, dan semesta tak terhitung. Anak kecil itu menyimpan jagat raya.
- Visual: Surreal wondrous cosmic interior: the tiny black crow floating in deep space surrounded by countless spiralling galaxies, many suns and moons and miniature worlds, framed by the faint gentle glowing silhouette of a smiling child's face around the edges
- Karakter: RAMA_KECIL
- Kamera: slow floating zoom-out with a slight rotation

## S15 · Masa muda (kisah)
- VO: Rama tumbuh mahir memanah, berkuda, dan mengendalikan gajah. Ia juga belajar kitab suci. Kuat, tapi tenang. Pintar, tapi tidak suka pamer.
- Visual: Royal training ground at golden morning: teenage Rama draws his bow with perfect calm posture toward a distant target, behind him a decorated royal elephant and a white horse, instructors watching, banners fluttering
- Karakter: RAMA
- Kamera: slow side dolly past Rama toward the target

## S16 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Suatu hari, resi Wiswamitra datang ke istana. Ritual sucinya terus diganggu raksasa suruhan Rahwana. Ia meminta satu bantuan: pinjamkan Rama.
- Visual: Grand throne hall with tall carved pillars: Wiswamitra stands before the throne with staff raised in earnest request, Dasarata rising from his golden throne to welcome him, courtiers, shafts of daylight
- Karakter: WISWAMITRA, DASARATA
- Kamera: slow push-in down the throne hall

## S17 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Dasarata gemetar. Rama belum genap enam belas tahun. Sang raja menawarkan pasukannya, bahkan dirinya sendiri. Akhirnya Wasista, guru istana, meyakinkannya untuk melepas Rama.
- Visual: Throne hall close composition: anxious Dasarata with trembling hands on young Rama's shoulders, Rama looking back calmly, beside them a second elderly sage with a neat white beard and white robes, the royal priest Wasista, gently advising
- Karakter: DASARATA, RAMA
- Kamera: slow push-in on the three faces

## S18 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Di tepi sungai Sarayu, Rama menerima dua mantra, Bala dan Atibala. Penangkal lelah, lapar, dan haus. Malam itu, pangeran istana tidur beralas rumput.
- Visual: Blue hour by a wide sacred river: Wiswamitra seated teaching, Rama kneeling with closed eyes while soft two threads of golden light swirl around him, Lakshmana beside; a simple bed of grass nearby, fireflies, reeds, stars
- Karakter: RAMA, LAKSMANA, WISWAMITRA
- Kamera: slow arc around the kneeling Rama

## S19 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Lalu mereka memasuki hutan gelap milik Tataka. Dulu ia makhluk penjaga alam, lalu kutukan mengubahnya menjadi raksasi. Seluruh wilayah itu ditinggalkan warga.
- Visual: A vast eerie dark forest with twisted giant trees, broken abandoned village huts overgrown with vines, mist; Rama, Lakshmana and Wiswamitra walk cautiously on a path, huge shadowy footprints, a faint pair of amber eyes deep in the darkness
- Karakter: RAMA, LAKSMANA, WISWAMITRA
- Kamera: slow forward tracking behind the three travellers

## S20 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Rama ragu, sebab lawannya perempuan. Gurunya mengingatkan, melindungi rakyat adalah tugas. Rama menarik busurnya. Tataka tumbang, dan hutan itu bebas lagi.
- Visual: Dramatic dusk clearing: Tataka looms large in swirling dust and leaves on one side, Rama on the other side stands firm with bow fully drawn and a glowing arrow tip, distance between them, heroic tension, no blood
- Karakter: RAMA, TATAKA
- Kamera: slow dramatic push-in from Rama toward Tataka

## S21 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Wiswamitra pun mewariskan senjata-senjata langit. Dalam kisahnya, senjata itu muncul bercahaya, lalu berjanji melayani Rama. Rama menerimanya dengan hormat.
- Visual: Mountain clearing at sunrise: Wiswamitra raises his hands as a circle of radiant celestial weapons made of light, a spinning discus, a golden mace, a trident-like spear, glowing arrows, hover around Rama who receives them with folded hands
- Karakter: RAMA, WISWAMITRA
- Kamera: slow upward spiral around Rama

## S22 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Kemudian Rama dan Laksmana menjaga ritual enam hari enam malam. Hampir tanpa tidur. Pada hari keenam, langit mendadak gelap. Marica dan Subahu menyerang.
- Visual: Forest hermitage at night turning stormy: sages seated around a sacred fire altar, Rama and Lakshmana stand guard back to back with bows, sky suddenly darkening with swirling purple storm clouds and two distant demon silhouettes approaching
- Karakter: RAMA, LAKSMANA, WISWAMITRA
- Kamera: slow pull-back revealing the sky

## S23 · Ujian pertama (kisah)
- VO: Subahu gugur. Marica dihantam panah sakti, terlempar jauh ke tengah lautan, tapi selamat. Ingat namanya. Kita akan bertemu dia lagi.
- Visual: Epic action wide shot: Rama releases a blazing blue-white arrow of energy that sweeps a large demon with curved horns away through the sky toward a distant stormy ocean on the horizon; hermitage fire below, no blood, no injury
- Karakter: RAMA
- Kamera: fast-feeling but smooth wide pan following the arrow of light

## S24 · Jalan ke Mithila (kisah)
- VO: Dalam perjalanan, mereka singgah di pertapaan sunyi. Di sana Ahalya menjalani kutukan suaminya, seorang resi. Ia hidup menyepi, tak terlihat siapa pun.
- Visual: Silent overgrown hermitage garden with a small abandoned hut, pale dried leaves and ash on the ground; Rama, Lakshmana and Wiswamitra arrive at the entrance; barely visible shimmering translucent outline of a seated woman meditating inside soft mist
- Karakter: RAMA, LAKSMANA, WISWAMITRA
- Kamera: slow cautious dolly through the hermitage entrance

## S25 · Jalan ke Mithila (kisah)
- VO: Begitu Rama datang, Ahalya tampak kembali. Kedua pangeran menyentuh kakinya dengan hormat. Kutukan berakhir. Di cerita populer ia jadi batu, tapi Walmiki tidak begitu.
- Visual: The same hermitage now blooming with flowers: Ahalya, a serene woman in plain white sari, becomes visible in warm golden light as Rama and Lakshmana kneel respectfully touching her feet, petals falling from the sky
- Karakter: RAMA, LAKSMANA
- Kamera: slow gentle push-in on Ahalya

## S26 · Mithila (versi)
- VO: Di versi Tulsidas, ada momen manis di taman kerajaan Mithila. Rama sedang memetik bunga untuk gurunya. Sinta datang berdoa. Mata mereka bertemu.
- Visual: Lush royal garden in spring with a lotus pond with jewelled steps, peacocks, parrots and vine pavilions: Rama holding a basket of flowers on one side, Sinta with two friends arriving near a small white temple on the other side, their eyes meeting across the pond
- Karakter: RAMA, SINTA
- Kamera: slow lateral glide across the lotus pond

## S27 · Mithila (kisah)
- VO: Siapa Sinta? Menurut Walmiki, Raja Janaka menemukannya sebagai bayi di alur tanah saat membajak. Nama Sinta sendiri berarti alur bajak.
- Visual: Golden sunrise over a freshly ploughed field: Janaka kneels beside a wooden plough drawn by white oxen and gently lifts a glowing baby girl wrapped in rose cloth from the earth furrow, flowers sprouting around, attendants amazed
- Karakter: JANAKA
- Kamera: slow low push-in along the furrow

## S28 · Busur Siwa (kisah)
- VO: Janaka punya syarat. Sinta hanya untuk pria yang sanggup memasang tali pada busur Siwa. Banyak raja mencoba. Mengangkatnya saja mereka gagal.
- Visual: Immense royal hall: a colossal ornate ancient bow resting on a stone platform, several proud kings in colourful armour straining together and failing to lift it, Janaka watching from his seat
- Karakter: JANAKA
- Kamera: slow push-in on the straining men and the giant bow

## S29 · Busur Siwa (kisah)
- VO: Para raja yang malu lalu mengepung Mithila selama setahun. Janaka bertapa memohon bantuan. Para dewa mengirim pasukan, dan pengepungan itu bubar.
- Visual: Wide aerial view of the walled city of Mithila surrounded by army camps with banners at dusk, while a luminous celestial army of golden warriors descends from the clouds scattering the camps; Janaka on a tower with folded hands in foreground
- Karakter: JANAKA
- Kamera: slow aerial drift toward the city

## S30 · Busur Siwa (kisah)
- VO: Kini giliran Rama. Busur itu dibawa dalam peti besi beroda delapan. Dalam kisahnya, lima ribu orang bertubuh kuat harus menariknya.
- Visual: A huge iron chest on eight great wooden wheels being pulled by a long crowd of many strong men with thick ropes into a magnificent hall; Rama, Wiswamitra and Janaka waiting, onlookers on balconies
- Karakter: RAMA, JANAKA, WISWAMITRA
- Kamera: steady tracking beside the great chest

## S31 · Busur Siwa (kisah)
- VO: Rama mengangkatnya, memasang tali, lalu menariknya. Busur itu patah. Suaranya seperti gunung runtuh. Hampir semua orang di balairung jatuh pingsan.
- Visual: The climactic moment: Rama standing centre bending the colossal bow which snaps in the middle with a burst of golden light and shockwave, courtiers around reeling and falling gently, Sinta watching from a balcony with amazed eyes, banners blown back
- Karakter: RAMA, SINTA
- Kamera: slow push-in, then one gentle shake at the break

## S32 · Pernikahan (kisah)
- VO: Janaka menyerahkan Sinta, yang akan mendampingi Rama seperti bayangan. Di hari yang sama, ketiga adik Rama menikah. Empat pasangan mengelilingi api suci.
- Visual: Festive wedding pavilion at night: exactly four bridal couples with flower garlands around one sacred fire, Rama and Sinta as the nearest couple on the right, Lakshmana and the other couples around the fire, marigold garlands, lamps, guests seated far behind
- Karakter: RAMA, SINTA, LAKSMANA
- Kamera: very slow push-in toward the sacred fire

## S33 · Parasurama (kisah)
- VO: Pulang ke Ayodhya, rombongan dihadang Parasurama, resi pejuang berkapak. Ia menantang: kalau memang hebat, coba tarik busur Wisnu milikku.
- Visual: Mountain road with a wedding procession of chariots and elephants halted: Parasurama blocks the way, axe on shoulder, holding out a great divine bow toward Rama, wind and dust, dramatic clouds
- Karakter: PARASURAMA, RAMA
- Kamera: slow push-in past the halted chariots to Parasurama

## S34 · Parasurama (kisah)
- VO: Rama menariknya dengan mudah. Tapi ia tak mau memanah seorang resi. Panah itu diarahkan ke pahala tapanya. Parasurama mengakui Rama, lalu pergi ke gunung.
- Visual: Rama holds the great divine bow fully drawn with a radiant arrow aimed upward toward a glowing golden heavenly realm in the sky, Parasurama beside him bowing his head humbly with palms joined
- Karakter: PARASURAMA, RAMA
- Kamera: slow tilt from Rama's face up along the arrow to the sky

## S35 · Kebijaksanaan (kisah)

- Visual: Bright Ayodhya street morning: Rama smiling greets first an old potter and a flower-seller woman with folded hands, they smile back warmly, colourful market stalls, children

- Kamera: gentle walking dolly alongside Rama

## S36 · Kebijaksanaan (kisah)

- Visual: Under a huge banyan tree in a palace garden at late afternoon: Rama sits on the ground listening attentively to three white-haired elders and sages, his bow and arrows set aside beside a practice target

## S37 · Kebijaksanaan (kisah)
- VO: Pada warga, ia bertanya soal keluarga dan pekerjaan mereka. Ikut sedih saat mereka susah. Seperti atasan yang hafal kabar timnya, tanpa dibuat-buat.
- Visual: Evening in a humble neighbourhood lane with oil lamps: Rama sits on a low step with a family of farmers, listening with concern, an elderly grandmother holding his hand, children peeking
- Karakter: RAMA
- Kamera: slow eye-level push-in on the family
